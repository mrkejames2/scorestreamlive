OUR MILESTONE WORKFLOW

M13-A → M13-B → M13-C → ... → Final Acceptance

For every sub-milestone:

Identify what existing components must remain untouched.
Create/continue the appropriate milestone branch.

Run cumulative regression against previously accepted functionality.
